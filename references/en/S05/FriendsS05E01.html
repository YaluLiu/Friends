Written by: Seth Kurland
Transcribed by: Eric Aasen

The text is black is from the original NBC broadcast of the show.

The text in blue was not present in the original NBC broadcast. It was added for the extended DVD version.

The text in red was not present in the original NBC broadcast or the extended DVD version. It was added for a special UK release, known as Friends: The Whole London Wedding.

The text in green was not present in the original NBC broadcast, but is found in both the extended DVD version and the UK release of Friends: The Whole London Wedding.
