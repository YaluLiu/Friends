Transcripts of Friends Episodes (including extended scenes)

Rescue This Website! || Return to Friends Homepage

Page created May 2026

These HTML transcripts represent countless hours of work by fans of the show. Initially, they were transcribed from the original USA broadcast episodes on NBC from September 1994 to May 2004. These transcripts preserved knowledge of the original NBC broadcasts, which often get argued about, because different versions of the show exist.

When Friends was released on DVD, the US boxed sets said "Now with footage you've never seen". These have been released in many other countries as well. These have become known as the "extended versions". The transcripts were then expanded to include the extra content. Typically, the original dialogue is presented in black text and the extended dialogue is shown in blue. But in a few cases, the color codes vary. Check the description at the top of each transcript.

Blu-ray and streaming editions of the show have been remastered based on the original USA / NBC broadcasts. They do not contain the extras lines and scenes which DVDs contain. With only a few exceptions, the Blu-ray and streaming editions match the original NBC broacasts.

Copies of the original NBC broadcast transcripts exist in many places on the internet. But for years, the extended transcripts were only found on one site (which now appears to be gone). And for 20+ years, only seasons 1-8 were completed in extended form. But I recently decided to do the work for seasons 9 and 10. I also did text refinements in all seasons, so simple search tools such as grep would have an easier time.

Thus, I'm happy to publish, for the first time anywhere, the extended transcripts of all 10 seasons! And I would be happy for anyone to republish these as well.
