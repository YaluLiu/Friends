[Scene: Central Perk, open mic night, Ross is at the small table, Chandler 
  is sitting on a barstool that he has pulled beside the couch, Phoebe, Monica

  y’know? She’s more (makes excited motions with his hands) ... y’know, and ...

  Phoebe’s "different style", he wilts under Phoebe’s icy glare)
