[Scene: Monica and Rachel’s, Monica’s sitting on the couch, Joey is rummaging 
  through her refrigerator, finally pulling out a full jar of olives]
Joey : Hey, how much you give me to eat this whole jar of olives?

  massages outside the UN, and ... I don’t know where his country is. 
Monica : Okay, let’s start with the free massages at the UN. 
Phoebe : Oh! That’s my new thing. I figure, bodies at peace make peace. 
  (beaming proudly)

  So what country’s this guy from?

  the door to fetch her globe)

  sophisticated, and he doesn’t speak any English, but, according to his translator,

  than a golf ball)

  up to her eye)
